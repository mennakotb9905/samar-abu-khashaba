01 — ABOUT

A scientist, an educator, a quality advocate.

Assistant Professor specialising in Pharmaceutical Analytical Chemistry, holding a PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences, a Master's in Pharmaceutical Analytical Chemistry and a Master's in Quality Management.

My work spans academic teaching, scientific research, analytical method development and validation, HPLC and pharmaceutical analysis. I have published in international Q1 and Q2 journals, with research interests in green analytical chemistry, sustainable method development, analytical performance and chemometrics.

In the lecture hall and the laboratory alike, I aim to turn complex science into clear, visual and memorable learning — and to bring academic expertise, research capability and innovative teaching to a leading educational institution.

04 — RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Analytical science that is precise, and kind to the planet.

My research centres on analytical method development and validation, analytical performance, sustainable analytical practice, and the application of chemometric approaches to pharmaceutical analysis and method evaluation.

Published · Scientific Reports

Voltammetric determination of paracetamol in binary mixtures with aceclofenac or dicyclomine

The first voltammetric method for the simultaneous determination of paracetamol in two binary mixtures, without prior separation or electrode modification.

  • Simple, unmodified glassy carbon electrode
  • Validated according to ICH guidelines
  • Aligned with 13 UN SDGs — sustainability score of 76%
Published · BMC Chemistry

Eco-friendly HPLC-DAD determination of colchicine and dexamethasone

The first HPLC method to quantify colchicine and dexamethasone together, in laboratory-made combinations and in rat plasma.

  • Both drugs resolved in under five minutes
  • Validated to ICH and FDA bio-analytical guidelines
  • Greenness, blueness and whiteness assessed
