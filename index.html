01 — ABOUT

Scientific Reports · 2026 · Vol. 16

A multihued sustainable appraisal of the electrochemical method for synchronized micro-estimation of the household drug Paracetamol with Aceclofenac or Dicyclomine

Aya R. Ahmed, Marwa A. A. Ragab, Mohamed A. Korany, Samar Abu Khashaba, Sara I. Aboras

An eco-benign differential pulse voltammetric method on an unmodified glassy carbon electrode for the simultaneous micro-estimation of paracetamol in two binary mixtures.

  • Linear over 0.2–25 µg/mL (with aceclofenac) and 1–25 µg/mL (with dicyclomine), r > 0.9995
  • Validated to ICH guidelines and applied to tablet formulations
  • UN SDG alignment evaluated with the Need, Quality and Sustainability (NQS) index

Continuous improvement

The PDCA cycle

  1. Plan Identify the problem, study the process and set a measurable target.
  2. Do Apply the change on a small, controlled scale.
  3. Check Measure the result against the target and learn from the gap.
  4. Act Standardise what worked — then start the cycle again.

Six Sigma · DMAIC — as applied to medication administration errors

  1. D
    Define

    A multidisciplinary team identified the error types and the critical steps of the process against JCI standards.

  2. M
    Measure

    Incident reports were audited and process maps drawn to establish baseline performance.

  3. A
    Analyze

    Pareto, fishbone and regression analysis traced missed doses to administration during rush hours.

  4. I
    Improve

    More nurses trained to ease the workload, and the nursing workflow redesigned to minimise interruptions.

  5. C
    Control

    Control charts, a risk register and FMEA keep the process monitored and the risks ranked.
